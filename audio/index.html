IDection Audio

Your own music, wherever you keep it — on your phone, on your network or in your cloud storage — played in high resolution, bit-perfect through USB DACs, or sent to the speakers and receivers in your home.

Privacy Policy Support

Your sources

Music on the phone, network shares (SMB, WebDAV), DLNA libraries, and your own Dropbox, OneDrive, pCloud or S3-compatible storage, which the app only reads.

High resolution

FLAC, WAV, ALAC and more in high resolution, DSD and SACD discs, and bit-perfect output to USB DACs.

In your home

Send music to DLNA receivers and Chromecast, and with DLNA+ control the receivers on your network: volume, equalizer, inputs and their own queue.

No accounts, no servers

No sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your phone; your music goes straight from where you keep it to where you play it.
